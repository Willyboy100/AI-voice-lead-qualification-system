# AI Voice Lead Qualification System

An automation project designed to streamline lead qualification through AI-powered voice conversations, structured lead data capture, and automated sales follow-up.

## Overview

Businesses often spend valuable time contacting new leads, asking repetitive qualification questions, and manually updating their records.

This project explores how voice AI and workflow automation can help businesses collect lead information, assess prospect interest, and organize follow-up activities.

## Project Objectives

- Automate lead qualification conversations.
- Capture and organize lead information.
- Record qualification outcomes and prospect requirements.
- Route qualified leads for sales follow-up.
- Reduce repetitive manual lead-handling tasks.

## Planned Workflow

1. **Lead Intake:** Receive a lead's information.
2. **Voice Conversation:** Engage the lead and ask relevant qualification questions.
3. **Qualification:** Organize responses and assess the lead's readiness.
4. **Data Storage:** Save lead information and qualification results.
5. **Sales Follow-up:** Notify the appropriate team about leads requiring attention.

## Technology Stack

- **Vapi:** Voice AI and phone conversations.
- **Make:** Workflow automation and integrations.
- **Airtable:** Lead data storage and tracking.
- **OpenAI:** Potential conversation summarization and structured analysis.
- **Gmail / Slack:** Potential notifications and sales follow-up.
